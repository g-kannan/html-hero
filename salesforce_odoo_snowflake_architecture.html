REFERENCE ARCHITECTURE

Salesforce + Odoo to Snowflake Data Platform

AWS-managed extraction, transformation, validation, and loading into Snowflake for analytics-ready CRM and ERP data.

Source capture Salesforce and Odoo are pulled through managed connectors or Lambda workers, with raw payloads preserved before transformation.
AWS ETL control plane S3, Glue, Step Functions, IAM, KMS, and CloudWatch provide replayability, scheduling, encryption, and operational visibility.
Snowflake target Data lands through stages into raw, curated, and mart schemas for BI, finance operations, CRM reporting, and downstream analytics.
